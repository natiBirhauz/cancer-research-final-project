4. Core Mathematical Insights

  • Scale Invariance: The compression ratio scales dynamically with input dimensionality — compressing 2,778 vectors down to 32 modules (86.8:1) in the broad Discovery cohort, and 54 vectors down to 4 modules (13.5:1) in the focused Targeted cohort.
  • Anti-Contradiction Robustness: In both cohorts, strict binary sign enforcement ($+$ vs $-$) preserved 100% biological integrity, eliminating conflicting marker states across patients.
  • Variance-Guided Dimensionality Reduction: In both experimental designs, the top 2-3 deviation components explain the majority of tissue variance, enabling steep feature reduction with minimal R² penalty.
